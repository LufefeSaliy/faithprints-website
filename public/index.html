Faith · Craft · Cloth

Every piece pressed with purpose

Faith Prints CCB is a customised clothing business built on a simple belief — that what you wear can carry meaning. Working out of Sandton, we design and press custom apparel using DTF transfer and heat press techniques, turning a blank garment into something personal.

From church wear and family sets to everyday tees, hoodies and golf shirts, every order is handled with the same quality and craftsmanship, whether it's a single custom tee or a bulk order for your congregation, team or family reunion.

Bring your own design, choose from ours, or let us create one for you — we'll help you press it onto the garment of your choice.

What We Do

Services

Every service below can be ordered on its own or combined — send us your garment and design, or start from scratch with ours.

01

DTF Printing

Full-colour DTF transfers, sold by the roll or cut to size — A5 through A3 and beyond.

02

Heat Press

Precision heat-press application onto your apparel or ours, in sizes from pocket prints to full A3.

03

Custom Apparel

Tees, golf shirts, hoodies, tracksuits and shorts — supplied, printed and finished to order.

04

Church & Family Wear

Matching sets for congregations, families and events — built for bulk orders without losing quality.

05

Logo & Design

Original logo and artwork design, prepared print-ready for DTF or heat press application.
